Privacy Policy for Invoice Maker
Last updated: 12 October 2026

Invoice Maker ("the app") is developed by Muhammad Amjad.

1. Data we collect
We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device
The information you enter (your business details, logo, customers, products and invoices) and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send this data to any server.

3. Files you choose to save or share
When you save a PDF or a backup file, or share an invoice with another app (for example WhatsApp or email), that file goes to the location or app you choose. What happens to it there is governed by that app's or service's own privacy policy.

4. Logo and photos
If you add a logo, the app uses the system photo picker. The selected image is copied into the app's private storage. The app does not get access to the rest of your photos.

5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

6. Deleting your data
You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
