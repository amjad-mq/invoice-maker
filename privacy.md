# Privacy Policy for Invoice Maker

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

Invoice Maker ("the App") is developed by Muhammad Amjad ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information when you use it.

## 1. Information Collection

Invoice Maker does not collect or transmit your personal information to our servers. The App does not include user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking features.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into the App may include:

- Business name and contact details
- Business logo
- Customer names and contact details
- Product and service descriptions
- Invoice details, amounts, and dates
- App preferences and settings

This information is stored locally on your device for the App's functionality. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

## 3. Internet Access and Data Transmission

The App does not declare the Android INTERNET permission and is designed to work offline. It does not send your invoice information to our servers.

## 4. PDF Files, Backups, and Sharing

The App may allow you to generate invoices as PDF files, save files to a location you choose, create backups, or share files through other applications.

Files you choose to save or share are handled by the destination you select. For example, if you share an invoice through an email or messaging application, that service may process the file according to its own privacy policy.

We do not receive copies of files you export or share.

## 5. Business Logos and Images

If you choose a business logo or image, the App uses the Android system photo picker to let you select an image. The selected image may be copied into the App's private storage for use in your invoices.

The App does not need access to your entire photo library to perform this function.

## 6. Data Security

Your information is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any exported files or backups.

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free. We encourage you to keep backups of important invoices in a secure location.

## 7. Data Deletion

You can delete information using the App's available deletion features, including **Settings → Delete all data**, if available in your version of the App.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its private local data. Files you previously exported or saved outside the App may remain on your device or in other applications and must be deleted separately.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Invoice Maker is a general-purpose business utility and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally transmit your invoice data to third-party servers. However, when you choose to export or share files using another application, that application's provider may process the information you share. Please review the relevant provider's privacy policy.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
